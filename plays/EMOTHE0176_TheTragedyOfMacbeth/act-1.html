456For thy undaunted mettle should compose
457Nothing but males. Will it not be received,
458When we have marked with blood those sleepy two
459Of his own chamber and used their very daggers,
460That they have done ’t?

LADY MACBETH

That they have done ’t? Who dares receive it other,
461As we shall make our griefs and clamor roar
462Upon his death?

MACBETH

Upon his death? I am settled and bend up
463Each corporal agent to this terrible feat.
464Away, and mock the time with fairest show.
465False face must hide what the false heart doth know.

They exit.
