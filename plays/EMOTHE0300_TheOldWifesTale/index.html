Note on this digital edition

With the support of research project GVAICO2016-094, funded by Generalitat Valenciana (2016-2017).

For this digital edition, the following alerations have been made with respect to Brooke and Paradise’s edition: the play’s title has be modernized as <<The Old Wife’s Tale>> ; speech prefixes have been expanded, as has the name “ <<Huan.>> ” in a stage direction on page 32; the speech prefix “[ <<Voice>> .]” at line 910 (page 35) has been corrected to “[2 <<Voice>> .]” in order to avoid the repetion of “ <<Voice>> .” in line 905.
