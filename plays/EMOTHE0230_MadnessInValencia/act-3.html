Scene 1

A room in the asylum. SANCHO and VERINO, a learned doctor.

VERINO

1665Then with Godspeed and a fair wind
1666her humours will trickle into place.

1668ever since we put her in the cage.

1669I suspected that from the outset.
1670She’s suffering from atrophy,
1671collapse through lack of nourishment;
1672the stomach shrivels like a dry prune,
1673like a piece of fruit in the sun,
1674and then grows cold so the body
1675freezes in its extremities.

1677or even better, some warm bread,
1678and then plunge her elbows and feet
1679into bowls of boiling water.
1680There’s no doubt, that’ll do the trick;
1681she’ll be up and about in a flash.

SANCHO

1682Can you treat her melancholy?

VERINO

1684It’s a form of catalepsis,
1685when the melancholic humours
1686blow hot throughout the body,
1687like the sirocco from Algery.

1690which drives your niece’s waking thoughts.
1691Sorry, not delicately put,

1695leading to fits of wildest fury.

VERINO

1721She would return to her first sense,
1722Shattered by her late obsession.

1726with darkness and with solitude.’

SANCHO

1728Indeed. You mean...

VERINO

SANCHO

VERINO

SANCHO

1736There’s no question of a wedding night.

VERINO

1738Have no fear. Feed her obsession
1739and her sanity is assured.

SANCHO

1740Talk of the devil... and here’s his monkey.

VERINO
