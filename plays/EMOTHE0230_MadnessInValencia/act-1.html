Act I

Scene 1

A wood on the outskirts of Valencia, beside the city’s famous madhouse.

FLORIANO

1Valerio, I’m over here.

VALERIO

2I came as quickly as I could,
3as soon as your message arrived.

FLORIANO

4It took me four days to get here,
5riding hard from Zaragoza,
6with the devil himself behind me.

VALERIO

7Your face is the colour of death.

FLORIANO

8There was no one else I could trust...
9you’re the only friend I’ve got left.

VALERIO

10Tell me for God’s sake what you’ve done.

FLORIANO

11I...

VALERIO

I... What?

FLORIANO

I... What? I...

VALERIO

I... What? I... You what?

FLORIANO

I... What? I... You what? I... ah... killed

VALERIO

12Holy God in heaven! Murdered?
13You mean you’ve murdered someone? Who?

FLORIANO

14A man... are you sure we’re alone?

VALERIO

15Positive. Tell me, who was it?

FLORIANO

16A man who’de’ve destroyed me if...

VALERIO

17Floriano, stand still, will you,
18there’s not a soul for miles around,
19except the crazed creatures in there.

FLORIANO

20Try and put yourself in my place,
21every rustle, every murmur...

VALERIO

22Calm down and tell me who it was.

FLORIANO

23They’ll be scouring the whole country,
24I can’t even trust my own shadow.

VALERIO

25You’re in a worse state than your victim...
26you’re a mess of nerves.

FLORIANO

you’re a mess of nerves. I panicked.
27I stole a horse and we bolted
28from Zaragoza to Valencia,
29down lonely country tracks and lanes,
30begging bread from the odd goatherd,
31though I’ve been hungry for four days.
32That’s what happened. In a nutshell.

VALERIO

33Let’s see if we can crack the nut.
34Who was it?

FLORIANO

Who was it? If I’m seen, I’m dead.

VALERIO

35Some gentleman? No... not a priest?

FLORIANO

36In God’s name, I swear I don’t know.

VALERIO

37Then, my friend, you are dead indeed,
38for if you refuse to trust me
39and keep on playing foolish games...

FLORIANO

40All right, I’ll tell you who it was...
41He was a nobleman... a prince,
42a sort of prince.

VALERIO

a sort of prince. What sort of prince?

FLORIANO

43Prince Reinero...

VALERIO

Prince Reinero... Reinero! He’s...

FLORIANO

44I know... tenth in line to the throne.
45I’ve had four days to work it out.

VALERIO

46They’ll hunt you to the ends of the earth,
47Like hounds close down a fox.

FLORIANO

Like hounds close down a fox. Thank you.
48I knew I could rely on you.

VALERIO

49God knows, if I could give my life
50four yours, then you’d be a free man.
51If I could bring him back to life
52with the love that I have for you...
53but your sin is against God and man.
54Though danger can sharpen the wits
55And invention may save you yet.
56We must think. (Silence.) How dit it happen?
57How did you get past all those guards?

FLORIANO

58Let’s get one thing clear – I killed him.
59I did not assassinate him!
60It was bad luck, sheer misfortune
61that brought him and me face to face
62in the house of a certain lady...
63Celia... she’s got the loveliest eyes...

VALERIO

64More blood’s been spilt for the sake of love
65than in all man’s battles and wars.

FLORIANO

66Believe me, I’d no taste for blood...
67I just wanted to get away,
68but he flew into a fury
69and would listen to no reason.
70There were two guards waiting outside
71and he set them on me like dogs.
72I ran into a narrow lane
73and he came thundering after me.
74I knew that I was about to die
75and with the courage of the dead
76I drew my sword and turned round.
77His guards had no room to get past,
78and I ran my sword through his arm,
79and plunged it clean into his heart.
80He fell like an ox at slaughter.

VALERIO

81You’re absolutely sure he’s dead?

FLORIANO

82I drove my sword in to the hilt
83and felt his blood run down my wrist.
84Then I turned on my heels and ran,
85thanking God for my good fortune,
86for to stay would have tempted fate,
87and I heard a voice crying out:
88‘You’ll not escape us, you bastard;
89This is no ordinary man you’ve killed,
90but Reinero, a grandee of Spain.’
91My blood froze, though thank God not my feet,
92and long before the cold dawn broke
93I’d put ten miles between them and me.
94Fear can be the cruellest, sharpest spur.

VALERIO

95There’s no telling where they might be,
96they could be as far north as France
97or as far south as hot Seville.

FLORIANO

98Or waiting round the next corner.

VALERIO

99There’s nowhere in Spain you are safe,
100and there is no easy escape.
101Your sword is bigger than your brain.
102God damn your recklessness in love!
103I’m being pulled in all directions...
104a thousand possibilities
105and not a hope that’s hard and fast.
106How can I keep a king at bay?
107How could I hope to shelter you
108in a town full of prying eyes?
109There’s nowhere you won’t create a stir.
110Unless... unless... the asylum!
111Could you pretend to have gone mad,
112you know, ranting, raving, so on,
113so that they take you for a loony?

FLORIANO

114Do you think I’m not already?
115But I’ll not have any madhouse,
116I’ve seen the wretches they torture there.

VALERIO

117There’s an asylum in Valencia
118as famous as the world is wide,
119where all sorts of fits and frenzies
120are treated and cured with science.
121If you go into the madhouse
122Your enemies will think you dead.

FLORIANO

123In rags, in dirt, amongst the mad...

VALERIO

124I think you may well benefit,
125and besides, it’s your only hope.

FLORIANO

126Valerio, you’ve saved my life!
127I’ll rant and rave to such effect,
128even you will doubt my sanity.

VALERIO

129Just act as though you were in love.
130What greater madness is there?
