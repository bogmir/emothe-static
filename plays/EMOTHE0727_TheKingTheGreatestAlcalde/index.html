Note on this digital edition

This publication is part of the research project “Early Modern Spanish and European Theatre: Heritage and Databases”, reference PID2019-104045GB-C54 (acronym EMOTHE), funded by MICIN/AEI/10.13039/501100011033. This digital edition is an adaptation of the <<The King, The Greatest Alcalde>> , John Garrett Underhill's translation of Lope de Vega’s <<El mejor alcalde, el rey>> . Underhill’s translations was first published in 1936 as part of his collection <<Four Plays by Lope de Vega>> . All spelling and punctuation has been modernized using U.S. English spelling conventions.
