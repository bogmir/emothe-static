SCENE V.

HALL IN THE ROYAL PALACE.

BASILIUS and ASTOLFO.

BASILIUS.

2523Who can expect, Astolfo, to restrain
2524An untamed steed that wildly turns to flee?
2525Who can the current of a stream detain,
2526That swollen with pride sweeps down to seek the sea?
2527Who can prevent from tumbling to the plain
2528Some mighty peak the lightning's flash sets free?
2529Yet each were easier in its separate way,
2530Than the rude mob's insensate rage to stay.
2531The several bands that throng each green retreat
2532This truth proclaim by their disparted cries;
2533Astolfo here the echoing notes repeat,
2534While there 'tis Sigismund that rends the skies
2535The place where late the land was glad to greet
2536The choice we made, a second venture tries;
2537And soon will be, as Horror o'er it leans,
2538The fatal theatre of tragic scenes.

ASTOLFO.

2539My lord, let all this joy suspended be,
2540These plaudits cease, and to another day
2541Defer the rapture thou hast promised me;
2542For if this Poland (which I hope to sway)
2543Resists to-day my right of sovereignty,
2544'Tis that by merit I should win my way.
2545Give me a steed; to stem this wild revolt
2546My pride shall be the flash that bears the bolt.

[Exit.

BASILIUS.

2547Slight help there is for what is fixed by fate,
2548And much of danger to foresee the blow;
2549If it must fall, defence is then too late,
2550And he who most forestalls doth most foreknow.
2551Hard law! Stern rule! Dire fact to contemplate!
2552That he who thinks to fly doth nearer go.
2553Thus by the very means that I employed,
2554My country and myself I have destroyed.

SCENE VI.

ESTRELLA and BASILIUS.

ESTRELLA.

2555If, mighty lord, thy presence, which it braves,
2556The tumult of the crowd cannot defeat —
2557The frenzy of the multitude that raves
2558In hostile bands through every square and street,—
2559Thou'lt see thy kingdom swim in crimson waves,
2560A purple sea of blood shall round it beat;
2561For even already in its dismal doom
2562All is disaster, tragedy, and gloom.
2563Such is thy kingdom's ruin, so severe
2564The hard and bloody trial fate hath sent,
2565Dazed is the eye, and terrified the ear;
2566Dark grows the sun, and every wind is spent;
2567Each stone a mournful obelisk doth rear,
2568And every flower erects a monument;
2569A grave seems every house, whence life is gone,—
2570Each soldier is a living skeleton.

SCENE VII.

CLOTALDO, BASILIUS, and ESTRELLA.

CLOTALDO.

2571Thanks be to God, I reach thy feet alive.

BASILIUS.

CLOTALDO.

[Exit.

2770Its mighty armies led

2778I learn to my sad cost,

[A trumpet sounds.

CLARIN.

2782Upon a rapid steed,

SCENE X.

ROSAURA, in a light corselet, with sword and dagger;

SIGISMUND, and Soldiers.

ROSAURA.

2801Noble-hearted Sigismund!
2802Thou whose hidden light heroic
2803Issues from its night of shadows
2804To the great deeds of its morning;
2805And as heaven's sublimest planet
2806From the white arms of Aurora
2807Back restores their beauteous colour
2808To the wild flowers and the roses,
2809And upon the seas and mountains,
2810When endiademed with glory,
2811Scatters light, diffuses splendour,
2812Braids their foam, their hair makes golden;
2813Thus thou dawnest on the world
2814Bright auspicious sun of Poland,
2815Who will help a hapless woman,
2816She who at thy feet doth throw her,
2817Help her, since she is unhappy,
2818And a woman; two good motives
2819Quite enough to move a man
2820Who of valour so doth boast him,
2821Though even one would be sufficient,
2822Though even one would be all potent.
2823Thou hast seen me thrice already,
2824Thrice thou hast not truly known me,
2825For each time by different dresses
2826Was I strangely metamorphosed.
2827First I seemed to thee a man,
2828When within thy sad and sombre
2829Cell thou sawest me, when thy life
2830Wiled from me mine own misfortunes.
2831As a woman next thou sawest me,
2832Where the splendours of thy throne-room
2833Vanished like a fleeting vision,
2834Vain, phantasmal and abortive.
2835The third time is now, when being
2836Something monstrous and abnormal,
