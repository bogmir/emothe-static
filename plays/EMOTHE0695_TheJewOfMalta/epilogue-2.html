Epilogue to the Stage

130In graving, with Pygmalion to contend,
131Or painting, with Apelles, doubtless the end
132Must be disgrace; our actor did not so:
133He only aimed to go, but not outgo.
134Nor think that this day any prize was played;
135Here were no bets at all, no wagers laid;
136All the ambition that his mind doth swell
137Is butt to hear from you (by me) ’twas well.
