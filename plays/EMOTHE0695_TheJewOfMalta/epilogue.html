Epilogue Spoken at Court

124It is our fear, dread Sovereign, we have been
125Too tedious; neither can ’t be less than sin
126To wrong your princely patience: if we have,
127Thus low dejected, we your pardon crave;
128And, if aught here offend your ear or sight,
129We only act, and speak, what others write.
