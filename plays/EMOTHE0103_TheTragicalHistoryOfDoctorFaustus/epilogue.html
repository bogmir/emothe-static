The Tragical History of Doctor Faustus

[Epilogue]

Enter CHORUS.

Chorus

125Cut is the branch that might have grown full straight,
126And burnèd is Apollo's laurel bough
127That sometime grew within this learnèd man.
128Faustus is gone. Regard his hellish fall,
129Whose fiendful fortune may exhort the wise
130Only to wonder at unlawful things,
131Whose deepness doth entice such forward wits
132To practise more than heavenly power permits.

[Exit.]
