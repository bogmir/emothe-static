MARCUS:

2Fuck him and his fucking empire,
3dragging us half-way across Asia

9With his son at home like a lord,
10with more tarts than the Queen of Hearts
11to make sure that he's all right jack,
12while we slog from pillar to post,
13in this god-forsaken country,
14to teach the Persians a lesson
15he thinks they'll never forget.

Curius:

17while that young bastard Carinus
18has the whole of Rome terrorised.

23one a coward and a bully,

MAXIMIAN:
