[Interlude 2]

Wife

70How dost thou like this, George?

Citizen

71Why, this is well, coney; but if Rafe were hot once, thou shouldst see more.

Wife

72The fiddlers go again, husband.

Citizen

73Ay, Nell, but this is scurvy music. I gave the whoreson gallows money, and I think he has not got me the waits of Southwark. If I hear him not anon, I'll twinge him by the ears. — You musicians, play "Baloo".

Wife

74No, good George, let's ha' "Lachrymae."

Citizen

75Why, this is it, coney.

Wife

76It's all the better, George. Now, sweet lamb, what story is that painted upon the cloth? The confutation of Saint Paul?

Citizen

77No, lamb, that's Rafe and Lucrece.

Wife

78Rafe and Lucrece? Which Rafe? our Rafe?

Citizen

79No, mouse, that was a Tartarian.

Wife

80A Tartarian? Well, I would the fiddlers had done, that we might see our Rafe again.
