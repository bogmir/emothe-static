[Act I, Scene i]

Enter Count LODOVICO, ANTONELLI and GASPARO

LODOVICO

1Banish’d?

ANTONELLI

2It griev’d me much to hear the sentence.

LODOVICO

3Ha, ha, O Democritus thy gods
4That govern the whole world! Fortune’s a right whore.
5If she gives ought, she deals it in small parcels.
6That she may take away all at one swoop.
7This ‘tis to have great enemies, God quite them:
8Your wolf no longer seems to be a wolf
9That when she’s hungry.

GASPARO

10Are men of princely rank

LODOVICO

11Oh I pray for them.
12The violent thunder is adored by those
13Are pash’d in pieces by it.

ANTONELLI

14You are justly doom’d; look but a little back
15Into yout former life: you have in three years
16Ruin’d the noblest cardiom –

GASPARO

17Your followers
18Have swallowed you like mummia, and being sick
19With such unnatural and horrid physic
20Vomit you up I’th’ kennel –

ANTONELLI

21All the damnable degrees
22Of drinkings have you stagger’d through; one citizen
23Is lord of two fair manors, call’d you master
24Only for caviare.

GASPARO

Only for caviare. Those noblemen
25Which were invited to your prodigal feasts,
26Wherein the phoenix scarce could scape your throats,
27Laugh at your misery, as fore deeming you
28An idle meteor which drawn forth the earth
29Would be soon lost I’th’air.

ANTONELLI

Would be soon lost I’th’air. Jest upon you,
30And say you were begotten in an earthquake,
31You have ruin’d such fair lordships.

LODOVICO

You have ruin’d such fair lordships. Very good,
32This well goes with two buckets, I must tend
33The pouring out of either.

GASPARO

The pouring out of either. Worse than these,
34You have acted certain murders here in Rome,
35Bloody and full of horror.

LODOVICO

Bloody and full of horror. ‘Las they were flea-bitings:
36Why took they not my head then?

GASPARO

Why took they not my head then? O my lord
37The law doth sometimes mediate, thinks it good
38Not ever to steep violent sins in blood;
39This gentle penance may both en your crimes,
40And in the example better these bad times.

LODOVICO

41So; but I wonder then some great men scape
42This banishment; there’s Paulo Giordano Orsini,
43The Duke of Brachiano, now lives in Rome,
44And by close panderism seeks to prostitute
45The honour of Vittoria Corombona:
46Vittoria, she that might have got my pardon
47For one kiss to the Duke.

ANTONELLI

48Have a full man within you.
49We see that trees bear no such pleasant fruit
50There where they grew first, as where they are new set.
51Perfumes the more they are chaf’d the more they render
52Their pleasing scents, and so affliction
53Expresseth virtue, fully, whether true,
54Or else adulterate.

LODOVICO

Or else adulterate. Leave your painted conforts.
55I’ll make Italian cut-works in their guts
56If ever I return.

GASPARO

If ever I return. O sir.

LODOVICO

If ever I return. O sir. I am patient.
57I have seen some ready to be executed
58Give pleasant looks, and money, and grown familiar
59With the knave hangman; so do I, I thank them,
60And would account them nobly merciful
61Would they dispatch me quickly.

ANTONELLI

Would they dispatch me quickly. Fare you well,
62We shall find time I doubt not, to repeal
63[A sennet sounds.] Your banishment.

LODOVICO

[A sennet sounds.] Your banishment. I am ever bound to you;
64This is the world’s alms; pray make use of it:
65Great men sell sheep, thus to be cut in pieces,
66When first they have shorn them bare and sold their fleeces.

Exeunt
