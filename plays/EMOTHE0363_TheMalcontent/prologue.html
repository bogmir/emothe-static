The Malcontent

Vexat censura columbas.

An Imperfect Ode, Being But One Staff, Spoken by the

PROLOGUE

136To wrest each hurtles thought to private sense
137Is the foul use of ill-bred Impudence:
138Immodest censure now grows wild,
139All overrunning.
140Let Innocence be ne'er so chaste,
141Yet at the last
142She is defil'd
143With too nice-brained cunning.
144O you of fairer soul,
145Control
146With an Herculean arm
147This harm;
148And once teach all old freedom of a pen,
149Which still must write of fools, whiles't writes of men!
