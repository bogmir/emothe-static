Act IV

Scene I

Fulvia, Samia

Fulvia

777Samia, oh, Samia!

Samia

778Yes, my lady.

Fulvia

779Come down here right away!

Samia

780Coming.

Fulvia

781Faster, for God’s sake. Faster!

Samia

782Here I am. What is it?

Fulvia

783Go quickly, find Ruffo the magician, and tell him to come here at once.

Samia

784Let me go upstairs and get my hat.

Fulvia

785Forget your hat, stupid! Go as you are. There’s not a moment to lose!

Samia

786(grumbling) What the devil is she so angry about? She’s on fire about something, and Lidio doesn’t seem to have quenched it.

Exit

Fulvia

787O false spirits and stupid human beings! How I have been deceived! How unhappy I am! I have harmed not only myself but also him whom I love even more than myself. Miserable me, who have what I asked for and found what I wasn’t looking for! If the spirit can’t help me, I am ready to kill myself: suicide would be better than a life of misery. Here comes Ruffo; soon I shall find out what my fate is to be. No one’s in sight, so I’ll talk to him out here. That’s better than indoors, for even walls have ears.

Scene II

Ruffo, Fulvia

Ruffo

788What is it, my lady?

Fulvia

789There tears, even more than words, show you how I feel.

Ruffo

790But what is it? Don’t cry, my lady.

Fulvia

791Ah, Ruffo, I don’t know if it’s my ignorance or your treachery that’s to blame.

Ruffo

792What are you saying, my lady?

Fulvia

793Whether it was my fault, or whether the heavens willed it, or whether the spirit acted maliciously –I don’t know the reason, but Lidio has been changed from a man into a woman. I discovered this when I was feeling him over. And though I have been deprived of my pleasure, I do not weep for my own sake so much as for Lidio, who because of me has lost that which he prizes most. Now you know the reason for these tears and will be able to understand what I want you to do.

Ruffo

794My lady, if I did not see these tears, which would be hard indeed to feign, I would have trouble believing you; but seeing that they are real, I can assure you that you need weep only for yourself. I remember you asked that Lidio visit you in the form of a woman, and now it occurs to me that the spirit, wishing to follow your instructions to the letter, sent your lover to you as a woman both in dress and in body. So cheer up, because he who could change him into a woman can change him back again into a man.

Fulvia

795This explanation makes me feel better already. If you can return Lidio to me whole, you shall have money, clothing, anything that is mine to give.

Ruffo

796I know the spirit will do as you wish, so I can promise you that he will change your lover back into a man immediately. However, in order to avoid making another mistake, state your wishes clearly this time.

Fulvia

797First, that he be given back the dagger for my sheath. Do you know what I mean?

Ruffo

798Quite.

Fulvia

799And that he visit me in the dress of a woman, not in the body of one.

Ruffo

800If you had said this earlier, this mistake would not have happened. But I’m glad it did, because now you know how powerful my spirit is.

Fulvia

801Ruffo, I beg you to relieve my suspense. I won’t have a moment’s peace of mind until I see him.

Ruffo

802You will be able to touch him as well as see him.

Fulvia

803When? Today?

Ruffo

804It’s late now. He’d only be able to stay with you a short time.

Fulvia

805He wouldn’t have to say. I would just want to make sure he was changed back into a man again.

Ruffo

806Ah, my lady, that would be like a thirsty man going to a fountain and being unable to drink.

Fulvia

807He’ll come tonight, then?

Ruffo

808If you wish. You can wait for him just inside the door.

Fulvia

809That’s not necessary. As long as he’s dressed like a woman, no one will know he’s not one.

Ruffo

810As you wish.

Fulvia

811Be of good cheer, Ruffo. You’ll never want for anything again.

Ruffo

812No more will you, my lady.

Fulvia

813And he’ll come right away?

Ruffo

814The moment I get home.

Fulvia

815I’ll send Samia along so you can let me know what the spirit says.

Ruffo

816Do so, and don’t forget that the lover, too, must have his reward.

Fulvia

817Don’t worry; he’ll have money and jewels aplenty.

Ruffo

818Go in peace. (Exit Fulvia) Love is painted blind with good reason, for he who loves never sees the truth. This woman is so blinded by her passion that she believes a spirit can make a person male or female at will, as if all that had to be done to make a woman out of a man was to pull out the root and replace it with a crack, and then, to turn him back into a man, sew up the mouth down there and replace it with a peg. Oh, the credulity of lovers! Ah, here comes Lidio and Fannio; I see they have already changed their clothes.

Scene III

Ruffo, Santilla as Lidio, Fannio

Ruffo

819You must get dressed up again.

Santilla

820Why?

Ruffo

821To go back to Fulvia. Ha, ha!

Fannio

822Why are you giggling in that obscene way?

Ruffo

823Ha, ha, ha!

Santilla

824Come, what is it?

Ruffo

825Ha, ha! Fulvia, believing the spirit changed Lidio into a woman, wants him to change back into a man again and revisit her.

Santilla

826What did you tell her?

Ruffo

827That it would be done right away.

Fannio

828You said the right thing.

Ruffo

829When will you go back then?

Santilla

830I don’t know.

Ruffo

831You don’t seem very enthusiastic. Don’t you want to?

Fannio

832Yes, we’ll go.

Ruffo

833You must be sure to do so, because I told her the spirit promised you would.

Fannio

834Don’t worry. We’ll go back.

Ruffo

835When?

Fannio

836Just as soon as we finish our errand, we’ll dress up again and go back.

Ruffo

837Don’t fail to do so. I see her maid standing in the door; I don’t want her to see me talking with you, so good-bye. Oh, wait! Fannio, let me have a word with you privately. This time, see that our dainty gentleman uses his pestle instead of his mortar with the lady Fulvia.

Fannio

838I’ll do that. Good-bye.

Scene IV

Fannio, Santilla as Lidio, Samia

Fannio

839Samia is coming out. Let’s step aside and let her go by without seeing us.

Santilla

840She is talking to herself.

Fannio

841Be quiet, and listen.

Samia

842This is what comes of meddling with the spirits: just see what they’ve done to Lidio.

Fannio

843She’s talking about you.

Samia

844First they made a woman out of him, and now they want to make a man out of him; this is a hard day for him and for me, too. But if they succeed, everything will be all right. I’ll soon find out, because my mistress has sent me to ask the magician. She is so sure of the outcome that she is getting together a sum of money to give to Lidio.

Fannio

845Did you hear that about the money?

Santilla

846I did.

Fannio

847Then let’s get ready to go back.

Santilla

848Fannie, you must be out of your mind. I don’t know what you could have been thinking about to promise Ruffo that we’d go back there.

Fannio

849Why can’t we?

Santilla

850Silly man, as if you didn’t know I am a woman.

Fannio

851So what?

Santilla

852So what, he says! Don’t you know, stupid, that if I reveal my sex, I’ll bring harm to myself, disappointment to Fulvia, and cause Ruffo to lose his standing with her? How can you prevent that?

Fannio

853You ask how?

Santilla

854Yes.

Fannio

855Where there are men, there are means.

Santilla

856But where there are only women, like her and me, the means are lacking.

Fannio

857Don’t worry. When I promised Ruffo that you would revisit the lady Fulvia, I knew what I was doing: I had it all figured out.

Santilla

858How?

Fannio

859Didn’t you tell me it was dark in her bedroom?

Santilla

860Yes, it was.

Fannio

861And that she did not talk with you but only felt you with her hands?

Santilla

862That’s right.

Fannio

863Well, I’ll go there with you, they way we did before.

Santilla

864Yes?

Fannio

865That is. I’ll go as a maid.

Santilla

866Well, and then?

Fannio

867Dressed as a woman, like yourself.

Santilla

868I understand that. But then what?

Fannio

869When you get in her room, pretend you have something to say to me. Come out of the room and take my place outside while I go inside and pretend to be you. She won’t be able to tell the difference in the darkness. She’ll think the spirit has changed you back into a man: we shall have all the money we want, and moreover I’ll be able to enjoy myself.

Santilla

870Fannio, I never heard of a cleverer plan than this.

Fannio

871So you see I was right to tell Ruffo we would go back.

Santilla

872Indeed you were. But in the meantime it would be a good idea to find out what’s going on at the house about this wedding of mine.

Fannio

873As to that, I see no way out: better not think about it in advance.

Santilla

874Putting things off is not going to solve them. They won’t look any better tomorrow than they do today.

Fannio

875Who can say? One thing at a time. Where the lady Fulvia is concerned we stand only to gain.

Santilla

876All right, but first go to my house and find out from Tiresia what is going on. Come back quickly, and then we’ll go to Fulvia’s.

Fannio

877I will.

Scene V

Fessenio, Samia

Fessenio

878What seems to be the trouble?

Samia

879My mistress has the devil in her.

Fessenio

880Ah?

Samia

881The magician changed Lidio into a woman.

Fessenio

882Ha, ha, ha!

Samia

883You think it’s funny?

Fessenio

884Indeed I do.

Samia

885It’s the gospel truth.

Fessenio

886You’re not serious, are you?

Samia

887Of course I am, stupid. And whether you believe it or not, that’s what’s happened. My mistress felt of him and found out he was a woman.

Fessenio

888Well, what’s she going to do now?

Samia

889You don’t believe me, so I’m not going to tell you.

Fessenio

890I swear I do. Tell me what she’s going to do next.

Samia

891The spirit is going to change him back into a man. I have just been to see Ruffo; he was given me this note to take to my mistress.

Fessenio

892Let me read it.

Samia

893Oh, no! Something might happen to you.

Fessenio

894Even if I should drop dead, I want to see it.

Samia

895Fessenio, be careful. Don’t meddle with the spirits.

Fessenio

896I’m not afraid. Show it to me.

Samia

897Don’t, Fessenio. Or at least cross yourself first.

Fessenio

898Come, let’s have a look.

Samia

899Well, all right... but you must promise to be as silent as the grave. If anyone found out I let you see it, it would be bad for both of us.

Fessenio

900Don’t worry. Give it here.

Samia

901Read it out loud.

Fessenio

902(reading) Greetings from Ruffo to Fulvia. The spirit, when he learned the effect of what he had done, was much amused. No one was to blame but yourself, but rest assured that he will restore to your lover the part that is now missing and send him back to you right away. He says moreover that Lidio loves you more than ever, so much so that he will never again give his heart to anyone else. But that you are not to speak to anyone of this, or a dreadful scandal might result. That, finally, you are to send money to him and also to the spirit, to show your gratitude both to him and to me. Be of good cheer, and do not forget your faithful servant Ruffo.

Samia

903Now you see it is true there is nothing the spirits do not know and cannot do.

Fessenio

904I can hardy believe it.

Samia

905I am anxious to bring this good news to my mistress.

Fessenio

906Go, and God be with you. (Exit Samia.) Good heavens, am I to believe that Lidio has really been changed into a man by magic, and that he will never love anyone again except Fulvia? Only God could work such a miracle, and yet Samia says that Fulvia felt him carefully. I intend to see this miracle with my own eyes, before he changes into a man again, and then, if it’s as she says, I’ll worship this magician forever. I’ll go to Lidio now; I may find him at home.
